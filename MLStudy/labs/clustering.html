Experiment conditions and how to read the results

240 synthetic requests, data seed 812, and component proportions [.45,.35,.20]. For independent standard-normal a,b, generate components as [−1.4+.65a, −.5+.5a+.6b], [1.2+.7a,1.1−.35a+.45b], and [.7+.4a,−1.35+.7b]. Coordinates are dimensionless, representing standardized request features.

Choose initial means from observations; initialize covariances to the full-sample covariance and mixture weights uniformly. Use full-covariance EM with 10⁻⁶ added to the diagonal. Stop when the absolute log-likelihood gain is below 10⁻⁶. Reject the update and stop if likelihood decreases by more than 10⁻⁵ or a nonfinite value occurs.

Color indicates the component with the largest responsibility, not a definitive assignment. The count of observations with maximum responsibility below 0.7 is also shown. This is an observational threshold, not model accuracy. Log likelihood measures fit to the current observations, not final predictive performance.
