03 / How to study

Predict first,
calculate, then explain.

If you know Python array basics, start with the first chapter. Keep the appendix nearby if the mathematics is unfamiliar, and write down why results differ from your expectations.

About these materials

The explanations and examples were written for this site. Models are computed on synthetic data and compared under changing conditions. Five browser labs and an advanced notebook with execution outputs are included, alongside observation conditions, interpretation limits, references, and code verification methods.
