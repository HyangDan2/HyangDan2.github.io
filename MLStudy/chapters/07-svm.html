The scores and distances related to 02 / are different from each other.

The sign of f(x) = wᵀx + b determines the class. w represents the normal line that is perpendicular to the boundary. The difference between the two points on the boundary, multiplied by w, gives 0. b is a scalar quantity that changes depending on the position of the boundary.

Key equationBoundary: wTx + b = 0
Significant distance: d(x) = (wTx+b)/‖w‖₂

If we multiply w,b by the same positive number, the score will change, but the boundary and distance remain the same. The score 1 is not equal to the distance 1. If it’s w = [2,0], b = −2, x = [3,1], then the score will be 4, and the distance will be 2.

The separable data is labeled with −1,+1. The scale is fixed so that the yᵢf(xᵢ) of the closest point becomes 1. The margin on each side is 1/‖w‖, and the overall width is 2/‖w‖.

import numpy as np
X = np.array([[-2,0],[-1,1],[1,1],[2,0]], dtype=float)

assert np.all(y*scores >= 1)
print("predictions:", np.where(scores >= 0, 1, -1))
print("margin width:", 2/np.linalg.norm(w))

Allow for overlapping observations of 04 /

When observations overlap, hard margin may not be significant at all. In the case of Slack ξᵢ, margin of error is allowed, and size is associated with costs. C represents the relative importance of risk and cost of margin of error.

Key equationmin ½‖w‖² + CΣiξi
yif(xi) ≥ 1−ξi,   ξi ≥ 0
Dongchi: ½‖w‖² + CΣimax(0,1−yif(xi))

ξ = 0 refers to the margin on the outside or above; 0<ξ<1 refers to the correct side of the margin; ξ>1 refers to the incorrect side of the margin. If C is large, the cost of intrusion becomes more important. However, data that cannot be separated doesn’t become a problem in hard margin.

05 / multiplier and support vector

If we combine the constraints with a factor of αᵢ ≥ 0, and then differentiate with respect to w,b, then w becomes the weighted sum of the observations. The sum of αᵢyᵢ becomes 0. By substituting these values back, we obtain the dual problem.

Key equationw = Σiαiyixi,   Σiαiyi = 0
maxα Σiαi − ½Σi,jαiαjyiyjxiTxj

soft margin is equal to 0 ≤ αᵢ ≤ C. The observations corresponding to αᵢ ≠ 0 are considered as support vectors. In soft margin, it’s possible to include observations that are within the margin or belong to different categories. Therefore, the statement that all such observations lie above the margin is generally not true.

The 06 / kernel and evaluation

By replacing the inner product of the dual problem with a suitable amount of semi-government-like kernels, it becomes possible to calculate the inner product of the characteristic spaces without directly creating characteristic spaces. The RBF kernel is equivalent to exp(−γ‖x−z‖²). γ changes its locality, and it is verified together with C.

A key feature of messages is that even when the same unit of measurement is used, the distributions can still differ. The scale is calculated during training and applied during verification. It’s important to distinguish between the precision·recall for each class and the matrix of confusion. The final score isn’t a corrected probability.

Will the margin increase infinitely if we reduce w to 0?

It’s necessary to satisfy certain constraints. If w = 0 is the case, then it’s not possible to satisfy both the margin constraints of different labels at the same time.

Is the test error always minimized when the maximum margin is reached?

The margin is a criterion for learning. The generalization ability should be evaluated separately, based on factors such as distribution, noise, and feature representation.

When learning is connected together: Internalization involves predicting and measuring distances; differentiation relates to the direction of learning. Probability, on the other hand, relates to the presence of uncertainty. Clearly defining model assumptions and verifying them through experiments is a common topic.
